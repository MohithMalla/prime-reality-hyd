## 6 Pages & Structure

1. **Home (`/`)**: Cinematic hero, consultative approach intro, 5 property category visual cards, core services highlights, 7-stage property journey timeline, 6 trust factors, and high-conversion CTA banner.
2. **About Us (`/about`)**: Corporate identity, mission, 5-stage consultative methodology (*Understand → Guide → Evaluate → Decide → Support*), core values, and the Prime Realty distinction.
3. **Services (`/services`)**: All 8 advisory practices (Property Sales, Investment Advisory, Property Consultation, Site Visit Assistance, Property Selection Support, Documentation Assistance, Channel Partner Support, After-Sales Support) with Lucide icons and deliverables.
4. **Property Solutions (`/property-solutions`)**: Deep dive into 5 property classes (Residential Plots, Luxury Villas, Modern Apartments, Farm Plots, Commercial Properties), plus the 6-factor decision advisory framework.
5. **Why Prime Realty (`/why-prime-realty`)**: 6 foundational trust pillars and the complete 01-07 chronological client roadmap.
6. **Contact Us (`/contact`)**: Registered address, direct phone/email triggers, business hours table, social links, and an interactive frontend enquiry form with live field validation and confirmation states.
